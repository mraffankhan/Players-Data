Profile

01 / SUMMARY

Affan Khan is an esports organizer who started as a staff member in 2021 and worked his way up to Tournament Manager, Tournament Director and organization owner. He runs events end to end: registration, scheduling, staff, creators, sponsors and the day itself.

On 10 November 2025 he started Ravonixx, which has organized six LAN events (24 to 120 teams) and four online tournaments with partner organizations. He also helps smaller esports organizations with planning, hiring and creator management, and leads Ravonixx’s marketing and social media.

Alongside esports, he works as a Graduate Engineer Trainee in IT at Presidency University, Bengaluru, and builds the tools behind his events: tournament management, Discord bot services and staff systems.
